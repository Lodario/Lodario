## Rollback

- Code-only regression with compatible database: promote the previous verified Vercel deployment.
- Broken optional surface: ship a reviewed fail-closed UI/API change or revoke the affected narrow RPC; never weaken RLS.
- Applied database migration: preserve migration history and prepare a new reviewed forward corrective migration.
- Data integrity/corruption: stop rollout, preserve evidence and current data, quantify new data after the recovery point, test the restore in isolation, then obtain explicit approval before restoring.
- Successful account deletion is intentionally irreversible through the app.

Use `rollback-plan.md` for decision thresholds and incident recording.

## Stop conditions

Do not deploy while any of these remains true:

- Either pending migration has not passed an isolated database execution and multi-identity RLS test.
- Backup/PITR capability or an isolated restore is unverified.
- The final dry run lists anything other than the two expected migrations.
- Linked database lint errors are unexplained or unaccepted.
- Any cross-user export/deletion/consent access, under-18 bypass, Guardian access, secret exposure, failed build, or health data in logs is found.
- Legal pages/operator identity, SMTP, Auth URLs, domain, or required environment variables are incomplete.
- The 30-day error/security log, 12-month feedback email, or 30-day manual-backup retention controls are not configured and verified.
- The full manual Player/Coach/mobile/time-zone test plan has not passed.
